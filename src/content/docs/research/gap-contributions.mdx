## Research Gaps

<div class="academic-placeholder-banner">
  <div><strong>Section Placeholder:</strong> Rigorous examination of existing shortcomings across static defense frameworks, manual ontology mapping, and disconnected severity metrics.</div>
</div>

*Content to be supplied: Exhaustive cataloging of research deficits in existing cyber-defense literature:*

1. **Gap 1: Disconnect between ATT&CK Threat Modeling and D3FEND Countermeasures:** While threat extraction to ATT&CK is common, automated algorithmic translation to verifiable D3FEND defensive primitives remains scarce.
2. **Gap 2: Lack of Information-Theoretic Severity Quantification:** Severity is predominantly scored via subjective CVSS numbers or static weights rather than empirical signature co-occurrence and mutual information.
3. **Gap 3: Absense of Dynamic Co-Evolutionary Optimization:** Defense strategies are evaluated statically rather than modeling the symbiotic co-evolution between evasive malware variants and defensive configurations.
4. **Gap 4: Unverified LLM Reasoning in Cybersecurity:** Language model inference often produces unstructured, uncalibrated estimates without strict schema boundaries and uncertainty quantification.
