## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why use CCEA instead of standard Genetic Algorithms?
Standard single-population genetic algorithms struggle to model asymmetric adversarial dynamics where both the attacker's evasion techniques and the defender's countermeasure selections mutate simultaneously. CCEA evolves both subpopulations symbiotically to discover robust defenses against evolving evasion permutations.

### Why is an LLM utilized in the severity scoring pipeline?
While graph heuristics identify candidate mitigations, qualitative domain reasoning is required to estimate whether a specific countermeasure (e.g., memory segment verification) effectively neutralizes a complex multi-stage attack variant or merely forces a bypass. The LLM provides structured, calibrated residual severity scoring governed by deterministic schemas.

### Can this framework operate in production SOCs?
The ultimate objective is to export Pareto-optimal defense configurations as actionable endpoint detection rules (Sigma/YARA) and policy configurations for deployment in production environments.
